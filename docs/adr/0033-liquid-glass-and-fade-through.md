# 33. Liquid glass tab bar and fade-through navigation

Date: 2026-10-14

## Status

Proposed (first draft, to be tuned by the owner on his phone). Amends [0019](0019-tab-shell.md) and the motion part of [0008](0008-theme-from-design-tokens.md).

## Context

The tab bar drew its frosted plate with a `BackdropFilter` blur. The owner wants a liquid glass bar, drawn with `liquid_glass_renderer`, the package his other app already uses. He also found the navigation abrupt: switching tabs swapped the indexed stack at once, the send flow swapped its views (search, review, sending, sent) at once, and a pushed route faded over `ColorScheme.surface` (kinu, the card colour) instead of the page background (washi).

## Decision

- `liquid_glass_renderer` is pinned at `0.2.0-dev.4`. The glass follows the iOS 26 Liquid Glass bar of Instagram, from two screen recordings the owner made on his iPhone (light and dark, 2026-10-20); it replaces the Figma effect style "Verre/Flottant", which drew a thicker frost. `FujinGlass.floating(brightness)` (`lib/app/theme/fujin_glass.dart`) is a thick, lightly frosted glass, so the content shows through and bends at the edges: thickness 28, blur 2, chromatic aberration 0.15, refractive index 1.2 and saturation 1.5 (the package defaults), light at -45°. Light: light intensity 1, ambient 0.5, for the whitened plate of the light recording. Dark: light intensity 0.4, ambient 0.2, for its smoky plate with a faint edge. The tint is a paint laid on the layer: `GlassSurface` paints `backgroundGlass` (kinu at 25% in light, `#2B2925` at 45% in dark) inside the glass. It is not the package's `glassColor`, which multiplies a dark tint into the backdrop instead of laying it over and made the dark plate darker than the page. `GlassLayer` (`lib/pages/common/glass_layer.dart`) holds one `LiquidGlassLayer` with that look and one `LiquidGlassBlendGroup` (`FujinGlass.blend`). `GlassSurface` (`lib/pages/common/glass_surface.dart`) is a `LiquidGlass.grouped` superellipse with a `LiquidStretch` and the outer floating shadow (`shadowFloating`, blur 20, y 6), plus a `GlassGlow` for glass buttons (`glows`); an optional overlay rides above the glass inside the stretch.
- The tab plate and each glass button have a rim: `GlassRim` (`lib/pages/common/glass_rim.dart`) strokes 1 px inside the edge with a linear gradient from top left to bottom right, `borderGlassHighlight` → `borderGlassShade` → `borderGlassHighlight` (the handles of the Figma stroke, at a quarter and three quarters). The dark highlight is white at 14%, softer than in Figma, as in the dark recording. The active tab's pill has no rim, like Instagram's.
- A glass button is `FujinGlassButton` (`lib/pages/common/fujin_glass_button.dart`, Figma component "Bouton verre"). `FujinGlassButtonSize.large` is a 68 drop with a 26 icon. `small` is a 40 drop in a 48 touch target with a 20 icon. The icon is an SVG drawn in `currentColor`. `onPressed: null` is the greyed state: the icon turns `textDisabled` and the semantics say not enabled. Its label is required. While held, a glass button swells to 115% (`FujinMotion.buttonSwell` on the `lensSwell` spring) and a white light (`backgroundGlassGlow`, 50% light, 20% dark) grows under the finger inside it (the package's `GlassGlow`). The swell changes the drop's size, not a paint transform: in this package version a `Transform` or `LiquidStretch` inside a glass layer moves the content but not the glass shape. The small button has its own lighter shadow (`FujinTheme.smallFloatingShadow`, 8 blur, 2 offset), since the tab bar's shadow left a halo around a 40 drop. When the icon changes, the old and new icons cross-fade with a quarter turn. The tab bar's Scanner (`ScanButton`) and the day/night toggle (`AppearanceToggle`) are both glass buttons. The button does not hold its own layer: the tab plate and the Scanner share one `GlassLayer` in `FloatingTabBar`, so they merge, while the Scanner of the search screen and the toggle each have their own.
- The tab bar follows the Figma component "Barre d'onglets". The plate has a padding of 6 and holds tabs of 87 × 56, 2 apart, each with a 24 icon, a 3 gap and its label. The Scanner sits 8 to its right. The bar is 353 × 68, 20 from the sides and 24 from the bottom. Nothing fades behind the bar any more: the page scrolls under it in full, as in the recordings, so the glass has content to bend. While a tab screen scrolls down by more than 24 px the bar shrinks to 86% about its bottom centre (`FujinMotion.minimizedBarScale`, on the `FujinMotion.barMinimize` spring); it comes back when the screen scrolls up by 24 px, reaches its top, or a tab is chosen. `TabShell` reads the `ScrollUpdateNotification`s of vertical lists. At 86% a tab is still 48 px tall, a full touch target (`FujinSize.touchTarget`).
- With the phone's Reduce Transparency setting, or high contrast, the plate and the drops become opaque `backgroundCard` cards with a `borderCard` outline, with no glass and no rim; they keep the shadow (`FujinGlass.reduced`). Flutter exposes no Reduce Transparency flag, so a native stream feeds `reduceTransparencyProvider` (`ReduceTransparencySetting`, `lib/data/accessibility/`, channel `dev.oznel.fujin/reduce_transparency`). On iOS it reads `UIAccessibility.isReduceTransparencyEnabled` and its change notification (`ios/Runner/AppDelegate.swift`). Android has no such setting. `ReduceTransparencyStreamHandler.kt` watches two keys. `Settings.Global` `accessibility_reduce_transparency` is the key behind Samsung One UI's "Reduce transparency and blur". `Settings.Secure` `high_text_contrast_enabled` is "High contrast text", readable by apps (`@Readable`); it is folded in because Flutter's Android embedding never sets `highContrast` (marked "NOT SUPPORTED" in its `AccessibilityBridge`). On iOS, high contrast ("Increase Contrast") arrives through `MediaQuery.highContrastOf`. So a stock Android phone and an iPhone each have a setting that turns the glass off.
- The active tab is a single `backgroundTabActive` pill (the Fūjin green, kept by the owner over Instagram's grey) that slides between tabs on the `FujinMotion.tabSlide` spring and widens a little with its speed. `TabPlate` (`lib/pages/tabs/tab_plate.dart`) adds Instagram's lens: as soon as a finger touches the plate, a clear glass lens (`FujinGlass.lens`, its own `LiquidGlass.withOwnLayer`, tint `backgroundGlassLens`: black at 6% in light, white at 16% in dark) swells 12 px past the tab on the `FujinMotion.lensSwell` spring and the pill fades under it. The lens follows the finger along the plate (`FujinMotion.lensFollow`), stretches up to 15% of a tab past either end against a resistance, and on release lands on the nearest tab, thrown 0.3 s ahead by the finger's speed (`FujinMotion.lensFling`); that tab opens. A tap opens its tab the same way. The lens also shows while the pill travels more than 0.3 of a tab after a tap (`FujinMotion.lensTravel`), and the pill is gone once the lens has swollen a fifth of the way (`FujinMotion.lensFade`). Under Reduce Transparency there is no lens: the pill itself follows the finger.
- The switch and the segmented control take a lens too, after two recordings of the iOS 26 Settings app the owner made (2026-10-21); only while a finger is on them, nothing at rest. The tab bar, the switch and the segments share `LensSwell` (`lib/pages/common/lens_swell.dart`): it swells the lens on the `lensSwell` spring while a finger is down or while the control is more than `FujinMotion.lensTravel` from where it lands, never under Reduce Transparency or high contrast, and fades the pill or thumb under it (`LensSwell.underneath`, gone at `FujinMotion.lensFade`). The tab bar and the segments also share `GlassLens` (`lib/pages/common/glass_lens.dart`, the `FujinGlass.lens` glass on its own layer) and `SlotRow` (`lib/pages/common/slot_row.dart`: equal slots, the finger's position with the resistance past the ends, the landing slot). `FujinSwitch` (`lib/pages/common/fujin_switch.dart`) replaces `CupertinoSwitch` and takes the iOS 26 switch's shape, measured on the owner's recording; only its colours are Fūjin's. Its track is 63 × 28 and its thumb is a 37 × 24 stadium, 2 inside the track, with no shadow. As soon as a finger touches it, the thumb fades and a 58 × 37 lens swells in its place, past the track above and below. That lens is `SwitchLens` (`lib/pages/common/switch_lens.dart`), not `GlassLens`: on the recording, the iOS lens shrinks everything under it to about 0.76 around its centre, so the track shows smaller inside with the card around it, and `liquid_glass_renderer` cannot do that (its refraction only bends near the edge, towards the inside above an index of 1, and breaks up below 1). `SwitchLens` is Flutter's `RawMagnifier` at the scale `FujinGlass.switchLensScale`. Over it sits a thin liquid glass layer (`FujinGlass.switchLensEdge`: 10 px deep, index 1.2, chromatic aberration 0.4). It bends what the magnifier shows along the rim and splits its colours, as on the owner's close-ups of the iOS lens, where the track curves into the edge with a coloured fringe. A reflection lies along the top and bottom edges inside the lens: `backgroundLensReflection` (sumi at 35% in light, white at 35% in dark) fading out over the top fifth and the bottom eighth (`FujinGlass.switchLensTopReflection`, `switchLensBottomReflection`). It was measured on the recording: in light a grey band under the top edge, in dark a light band under the top edge and a brighter one above the bottom edge. Then comes a 1 px rim from `borderLensEdge` at the top-left to `borderGlassHighlight` at the bottom-right and the small floating shadow outside it; a tap slides it to the other end on the `tabSlide` spring while the track turns from `backgroundNeutral` to `backgroundAccent` under it, and the thumb comes back as it settles. The thumb can also be slid (`lensFollow`), landing on the nearer end. `FujinSegments` gets the tab bar's lens: 8 px past the pill, following the finger and landing on the nearest segment, on Apparence and on the Mémoire segments alike; a drag that lands on another Mémoire segment turns the page. The control takes a `motion`: Apparence gives it `tabSlide`, Mémoire `InstantMotion`, since its position is already the page's. Reduce Transparency and high contrast keep the switch and the segments without a lens.
- Tabs switch with a fade-through. `TabSwitcher` (`lib/pages/tabs/tab_switcher.dart`) is the shell's `navigatorContainerBuilder`. Each branch keeps its navigator and state, as before. A hidden branch is offstage, out of the semantics tree and has its tickers stopped.
- Within a screen, `FadeThrough` (`lib/pages/common/fade_through.dart`) cross-fades views that replace each other. The send flow uses it between its phases. Searching and review are one view (`ReviewView`, `searching: true` while the plan is built): the header and the footer with the send button stay in place, and only the body cross-fades, without the scale, when the plan is ready. The owner found that swapping the whole screen there felt like a pop.
- The Mémoire segments float in the `HeaderLayout` header over the `PageView`, and the pages scroll under them ([ADR 0037](0037-progressive-blur-under-fixed-headers.md)). How a list ends under a fixed header (first a fade of the page colour) is now decided by [ADR 0037](0037-progressive-blur-under-fixed-headers.md): it scrolls under the header through a progressive blur.
- Titles that switch text keep their height: the Journal title row ("Aujourd'hui" or the month, with "Aujourd'hui ›" or "Définir mes objectifs ›" at its far end) is at least a touch target tall, so choosing another day changes the words only.
- The read-only sheets "Détail du jour" and "Ma journée" open with `SheetHeader` (`lib/pages/common/sheet_header.dart`): the title, the date under it, and top right the round close button of the flow headers instead of the Figma "Fermer" text. The table starts below, with room between.
- Pushed routes keep the platform transitions, with predictive back on Android and the swipe on iOS. On Android the fade runs over the page background.
- Every one of these animations follows the reduced-motion setting of [0031](0031-reduced-motion.md): `FujinMotion.of` for the motor builders, a zero duration for `FadeThrough`, and no stretch or press scale on the glass.

## Consequences

- The package only renders on Impeller. Without Impeller, as in `flutter test`, it falls back to its `FakeGlass` (a backdrop blur). Every build prints SkSL warnings for its shaders; they do not affect Impeller builds.
- The package is a pre-release, marked experimental by its author. Moving glass is redrawn every frame, so the effect has a cost on low-end phones that the emulator does not show.
- The bar's look on the phone is the owner's call. The glass settings are tuned against his Instagram recordings and live in `FujinGlass`; the springs stay in `FujinMotion`. The Figma style "Verre/Flottant" and the variables `color/background/glass`, `color/border/glass-highlight` (dark) no longer match the app, and `color/background/glass-lens`, `color/background/glass-glow`, `color/border/lens-edge`, `color/background/lens-reflection`, `size/floating-shadow-small-blur`, `size/floating-shadow-small-offset`, `size/switch-width`, `size/switch-height`, `size/switch-thumb-width`, `size/switch-thumb-height`, `size/switch-lens-width` and `size/switch-lens-height` have no Figma variable yet (the switch sizes come from the iOS recording, not from the Figma component "Interrupteur"); the owner updates Figma or the tokens. The Figma section "Playground" (round buttons, pills, tinted glass buttons) holds trials that were not adopted: only the tab bar, the Scanner and the day/night toggle are glass at rest; the switch and the segments only while held.
- The `glass-blur` token no longer drives anything: the frost now comes from the preset. The `scroll-fade` size token no longer drives anything either, since the bar has no fade behind it.
- The Samsung key comes from public adb tuning lists, not from Samsung documentation. It was tried on the emulator by setting it through adb, not on a Samsung phone. "High contrast text" was turned on and off on the emulator, and the bar followed each change. The iOS side has not run on a device or simulator.
- The lens and the shrinking bar were matched to the recordings on the Android emulator only; the recordings are an iPhone, where the system draws the real glass.
- Tests: `test/pages/reduced_transparency_test.dart` (no glass, no lens) and `test/pages/tab_bar_test.dart` (sliding to a tab, the bar shrinking and coming back); `test/pages/appearance_toggle_test.dart` checks that the toggle stays put while the Journal scrolls under it; `test/pages/settings_page_test.dart` checks the lens of a held switch, sliding the switch, that a tap on the row's title or note leaves it alone and sliding Apparence to "Sombre", and that Reduce Transparency leaves both without a lens.
