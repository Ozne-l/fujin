# 43. Edit and delete a logged food

Date: 2026-10-28

## Status

Accepted. Extends [0002](0002-write-ordering.md) to the edit and the delete of a logged food, moves the undo of [0026](0026-adding-a-food.md) to `RemoveEntryService`, and amends [0008](0008-theme-from-design-tokens.md) for the swipe's package motion. The design note `docs/notes/edit-and-delete-a-logged-food.md` holds the write plan, the halfway states and the owner's answers.

## Context

The Journal shows what the owner logged but cannot change it: a wrong quantity, meal or day means opening MyFitnessPal, then waiting for the next send to rewrite Ekklo. The mockups K21 to K30 and A08a to A08g, validated on 2026-10-27, add a swipe to delete a row and a tap to edit it in the add fiche.

Neither service can edit in place what Fūjin needs. MyFitnessPal has no usable edit of a diary entry in `myfitnesspal_client`; it adds (`diary.addFood`, which returns the new id) and removes (`diary.remove`). Ekklo removes an item (`meals.removeItem`), appends items (`meals.appendItems`) and changes the quantity of an item in place (`meals.updateItemQuantity`); `ekklo_client` has no move of an item to another meal or day. The send link ties a MyFitnessPal entry id to an Ekklo item, so an edit always gives the entry a new id.

## Decision

The gesture:

- A tap on a Journal row opens the add fiche in edit mode ("Modifier l’aliment", route `FujinRoute.editFood`, `EditFoodPage` with the `FoodViewMode` `EditingFood`). A swipe left uncovers "Supprimer". Nothing is deleted without the K24 sheet.
- The swipe is `flutter_slidable` 4.0.3 (pub.dev, MIT), pinned exactly, used by `SwipeRow` only, with the settings of the owner's reference: end pane, `StretchMotion`, `extentRatio` 0.35, a `DismissiblePane` at the package's 0.75 that opens the sheet and never removes (`confirmDismiss` returns false, `closeOnCancel` closes the row). `SlidableAutoCloseBehavior` (`SwipeRow.group`) keeps one row open per day; a `TapRegion` closes it on a tap elsewhere. Each `Slidable` is keyed by its entry id, as `DismissiblePane` requires, and clipped to the row, since the package clips only its pane and the row would otherwise slide over the card's padding and edge.
- The button is Fūjin's `RowAction` (the Figma component "Action de ligne"), not the package's `CustomSlidableAction`, which would take the theme's glass. It fills the uncovered strip, 16 px (`FujinSpace.s4`) from the row as in Figma, and stretches with the finger; the reference has no gap, but on the phone the row's end then ran into the button. It is as tall as the space between the two dividers: the `FujinSpace.s3` above and below a row is padding inside the row, not spacing between rows, so it slides with the row; its content fades in with the opening. Icon `TrashIcon` at `FujinSize.rowActionIcon` (token `size/row-action-icon`, 20).
- Every way into K24 (the button, the long swipe, the "Supprimer" semantics action, the fiche's "Supprimer") goes through `showRemoveSheet`, which fires `HapticFeedback.mediumImpact`. The row closes as the sheet opens.
- K24 waits for the delete: primary as a spinner, both buttons disabled (`SheetFrame.busy`), then closes on K25 or K30. Its alert finish comes from `SheetFrame.primaryFinish` (`GlassFinish.alert`), which only K24 passes; "Garder" and the trash pictogram are its own. K24 is its own wrapper rather than `showConfirmSheet`, because that sheet closes before it acts and its Mémoire callers pop the page after it.
- A sheet whose primary button is loading cannot be closed and takes no input, for every sheet built on `SheetFrame`: while `busy`, `SheetFrame` blocks back and the tap outside (`PopScope`), absorbs the drag down over the whole sheet, handle included, and ignores taps on its content. `SheetFrame` draws the drag handle itself (`showDragHandle: false`), since Flutter's modal sheet closes on a drag through `Navigator.pop`, which `PopScope` does not stop, and its own handle sits outside the sheet's content; a sheet opened with `SheetFrame.show` whose content is not a `SheetFrame` (the day picker, "Ma journée", the day detail) wraps it in `SheetFrame.handled`. When not busy, a drag down, a tap outside or back closes the sheet as "Garder" does. While a delete runs, K24 shows busy for any row, so a second delete cannot start.
- The removed row vanishes and the rows below rise one after another (`MealRows`, `FujinMotion.rowRemoval` with `rowRemovalStagger`, motor). The slide of the row itself is the package's animation, not motor; it follows reduced motion through Flutter. This is the amendment to [0008](0008-theme-from-design-tokens.md), in the same way as platform motion.
- Rows with nothing to uncover: offline, an expired session, a stale read, or a diary-only view (`DiaryMealCard`). A row whose entry has no id has no swipe and no fiche.

The writes, in the order of [0002](0002-write-ordering.md):

- Delete (`RemoveEntryService.remove`): a live read of the day; if the entry is linked (`InEkklo` or `ToUpdate`), the Ekklo item is removed and the link dropped (`SendService.removeLinked`); then the MyFitnessPal entry. Outcomes `Removed(ekkloRemoved)`, `StillInMfp`, `NotRemoved`. The undo of an add (K17, K18) uses the same service; `AddFoodService` has no undo of its own.
- Edit (`EditEntryService.save`): a live read of the day, the food re-read (`EditFoodChanged` when its version moved), the MyFitnessPal copy, the removal of the original, then the send flow's write for that one entry (`SendService.sendOne`): a quantity change on the same food and meal is a `updateItemQuantity` on the linked item, a change of food or meal removes the item and appends the new one, as the send flow does for rows "À mettre à jour". Outcomes `Edited`, `EditFoodChanged`, `NotEdited`, `EkkloNotUpdated` (with "Réessayer", `retryEkklo`), `OldEntryKept` (with "Réessayer", `finish`).
- A change of date removes the Ekklo item first, then copies in MyFitnessPal, then appends on the new day (decision 1 of the note), so no half-written state leaves an Ekklo item on the wrong day.
- A MyFitnessPal edit stopped after the copy: back to the Journal, both rows shown, a snackbar with "Réessayer" (decision 2).
- The link is never moved by hand between the two halves. `compareDay` shows the halfway state; the send flow writes the link once Ekklo is written.
- K26 "Annuler" saves the original values again through the same flow (`revert`), which gives the entry another new id.
- Rows "À mettre à jour" are saved in one go with the edit; a food outside Fūjin that cannot be read, or whose serving is gone, is refused by the fiche with a reason, and the swipe still deletes it; in edit mode the day picker stops at today; a newer food version opens the fiche with the A06e banner (decisions 7 to 10). Meal names use neutral forms, as the add flow (decision 12).
- Fūjin DEV: every write stopped by `ReadOnlyHttpClient` maps to `SendFailure.blockedInDev`, MyFitnessPal included (`MfpNetworkException`), so the add, the edit and the delete all show the DEV message (decision 16), without "Réessayer", as the send flow's automatic view already does.

## Consequences

- An edit costs a food read, live reads of the day and three to four writes. The edited entry moves to the end of its meal, since MyFitnessPal appends the copy.
- When the same food sits twice in a meal and only one of them is sent, an edit can attach the link to the other one; the next send rewrites it. A test pins this (decision 3).
- Known gap: on a change of date, if the Ekklo item is removed and the MyFitnessPal copy then fails, the fiche says `NotEdited` ("rien n'a été écrit") although the Ekklo item is gone; the Journal shows the row "À envoyer" and the next send writes it again. `edit_entry_service_test.dart` pins this.
- `flutter_slidable` is a new dependency; its slide motion does not go through `FujinMotion`.
- Figma still draws K21 to K23 as a fixed 104 px button 16 px from the row that stays open under the sheet; "Action de ligne" needs the 20 px icon variable. The trash icon in `assets/icons/trash.svg` is drawn by hand and must be checked against "Icône / Nom=trash".
- Where the code differs from the design note: `SendService.planOne` takes no `replacing:` and `EditEntryService` has no `plan`, since the fiche plans through `AddFoodService.plan` and `EkkloEdit` is computed in the page; the read-only text of A08g shows only when the connection or a session dropped after the Journal was read, because an offline Journal shows the last read's diary rows, which neither open nor swipe; K30 reuses the detail of K18 (`addStillInMfpDetail`); `SendService.sendOne` returns `null` once the entry is linked, else the error that stopped it, which `AddFoodService` and `EditEntryService` turn into their outcomes; `EditWriteNotifier` has only idle and running and returns each `EditOutcome` to its caller, which the fiche and the Journal act on, so no edit outcome is kept in state; `EditEntryService.open` does not reuse `AddFoodService._open`, which returns `null` both for an unreadable food and a missing serving and falls back to the food's first serving, while the edit must tell `FoodUnreadable` from `ServingMissing`, refuse rather than fall back, and report `OpenedOnNewVersion`; `Replaced` carries the replaced Ekklo food's name only when Ekklo gives the item's food, otherwise the fiche says "remplacé par" without it (`editEkkloReplacedUnnamed`, copy validated by the owner on 2026-10-28); the fiche in edit mode and its loading and refusal frames share one header, `EditFoodHeader`; a fiche whose entry is not in the day's settled read, or whose day failed to read, returns to the Journal on its own instead of waiting; in Fūjin DEV a blocked add shows `writeBlockedInDev` in the write panel without "Réessayer" (`AddWriteFailed.blockedInDev`, from `SendFailure.of`, as the delete does), and a blocked edit or undo shows it in a snackbar (`showWriteProblem`); a "Réessayer" after K30 keeps what the first try removed from Ekklo (`RemoveEntryNotifier.retry`), so K25 then says both sides; the K26 detail when only MyFitnessPal was written (`foodEditedMfpOnlyDetail`, "MyFitnessPal seulement · {serving}") is copy of Fūjin's own, which the owner validated at review on 2026-10-28.
- Tests: `FakeBackends.mfpRemoveFails` and `ekkloItemWritesFail` stop the delete and the Ekklo item writes; the fake's Ekklo append now files items by meal name and date.
