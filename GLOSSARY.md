# Fūjin glossary

The words below have one meaning in Fūjin's code, docs and conversations. French terms are the ones shown in the app.

## Domain terms

**Journal.** The main screen: one day of Fūjin's entries, copied in from the owner's MyFitnessPal diary when the day is read, in Fūjin's meals, with, for each entry, whether it is in Ekklo and whether either app still waits for it (connector status). Its pull, a change of day, a return to the foreground and the network coming back also start the background writer, and it shows the writer's failures as snackbars, and the replacements a fresh read made ("Remplacé dans MyFitnessPal"). Offline it shows Fūjin's own entries of the day from `fujin.db` (`FujinOnly`, K13), where foods are still added, edited and deleted. Code: `JournalPage` (`lib/pages/journal/journal_page.dart`), presenter `JournalNotifier` (`lib/pages/journal/journal_notifier.dart`).

**Day.** A calendar date with no time zone, stored as midnight UTC in memory and as `YYYY-MM-DD` in SQLite. The selected day defaults to today. A week runs Monday to Sunday. Code: `SelectedDay`, `selectedDayProvider`, `todayProvider` (`lib/pages/journal/selected_day.dart`); `CalendarWeek` (`lib/domain/journal/calendar_week.dart`); `CalendarDateHook` (`lib/data/database/calendar_date_hook.dart`).

**Entry.** Fūjin's own record of one food logged on a day, in a meal: a Food, the serving size logged (value, unit and MyFitnessPal's nutrition multiplier, copied rather than pointing at a `food_serving` row), a number of servings and the nutrients as logged, so that a refill of the food never changes a past day. Its grams are derived from the serving size when its weight is known, never stored. Each connected app may hold its own copy of it, a MyFitnessPal entry or an Ekklo item, tied to it by a MyFitnessPal link and an Ekklo link. Fūjin stores it first when the owner adds or edits a food in Fūjin, the background writer writing MyFitnessPal and Ekklo afterwards, or copies it in from MyFitnessPal when a day is read (copy-in); it keeps its Fūjin id through an edit, and a deletion, in Fūjin or in MyFitnessPal, soft-deletes it. Within a meal, entries are ordered by id, so a day copied in keeps the diary's order. Code: `Entry` (`lib/data/entries/entry.dart`), `LoggedEntry` (the entry with its food's name, brand and MyFitnessPal reference, its meal's MyFitnessPal name and its live links: what the comparison, the Journal and the send flow read, `lib/data/entries/logged_entry.dart`), `EntryRepository` (`lib/data/entries/entry_repository.dart`), table `entry`. ADR 0044. Not "log" or "record".

**Copy-in.** Storing, on a fresh read of a day, a MyFitnessPal entry whose id no MyFitnessPal link holds: a new entry and its MyFitnessPal link, the food found through its MyFitnessPal reference or created with the entry's description and brand (named only when it has no name, never filled, its reference without a version), the meal found through its MyFitnessPal mapping or created at the end. The other half of the two-way MyFitnessPal side: an entry whose values changed under the same id takes them, and an entry whose id left the diary is soft-deleted, its Ekklo link kept. A MyFitnessPal entry without an id is never stored: its row has no Fūjin id, no swipe, no fiche, and is never sent. A row is not copied in when the background writer still owes it: the row of an owed deletion, a row equal to a pending edit (its copy, written before the original was removed), a row a live link-less entry adopts (adoption of a written row); and an entry whose link was written after the diary was fetched is left as it is, never dropped (`readAt`). An entry with a pending write keeps Fūjin's values against the diary. Code: `compareMfpDay` (`lib/domain/comparison/compare_mfp_day.dart`), `CopiedFood`, `CopiedMeal` (`lib/data/entries/`), `FoodRepository.copiedMfpFood`, `MealRepository.mfpMeal`. ADR 0044.

**Food.** Fūjin's own food: its name and brand, serving sizes (`food_serving`, each with MyFitnessPal's nutrition multiplier), unit weights (`food_unit`) and the 21 nutrients MyFitnessPal gives, energy kept with its unit, all copied from the catalogue it came from, so that it can be logged with no connector. A food made from Memory or by a copy-in starts with no serving and no nutrient, and is filled the first time MyFitnessPal is read for it, from a search, a scan, the add or edit fiche, then filled again whenever such a read brings a MyFitnessPal version other than the one on its reference, its earlier servings forgotten; reads of the same version leave it as it is. Its name is null while it is known by a barcode link alone, and it is then shown as « Aliment sans nom ». After each Journal read in which MyFitnessPal answered, `FoodCompletionService` (`lib/domain/adding/food_completion_service.dart`) reads MyFitnessPal for every food with a MyFitnessPal reference and no serving, filling its name, servings and nutrients; a food it could not fill is tried again on the next such read, until it is filled. It keeps MyFitnessPal's verified mark (`verified`, null while unknown, then neither « Vérifié » nor « Non vérifié » is shown), written by every MyFitnessPal read that carries it. Code: `Food`, `FoodNutrients`, `FoodServing`, `FoodUnit` (`lib/data/foods/`), `FoodRepository` (`lib/data/foods/food_repository.dart`), table `food`. Not "product".

**Food on the phone.** A food that can be logged with no network: it has a MyFitnessPal reference with a version, at least one stored serving and nutrients, as foods added through Fūjin, or seen on a fiche, always have. Offline the fiches, the recents, the remembered foods and a scanned barcode link open it from its rows instead of MyFitnessPal; online MyFitnessPal is read as before. The offline search lists those matching the query, on name or brand, under « DÉJÀ DANS FŪJIN ». Foods copied in from the diary, or known only from Memory, are not on the phone until MyFitnessPal is read for them. Code: `FoodRepository.onPhone`, `FoodRepository.onPhoneFoods`, `knownFood` (`lib/domain/adding/known_food.dart`), `foodsOnPhone` (`lib/domain/memory/memory_rules.dart`).

**Reference.** The tie from a Food to the same food in one catalogue, by `kind`: `mfp` (a MyFitnessPal food id and version, at most one per food), `ekklo` (a matched Ekklo food and its name) or `own_copy` (an Ekklo own copy, per MyFitnessPal serving unit, with the version it was copied from). Memory's remembered foods are views of these references. Code: sealed `FoodReference` with `MfpReference`, `EkkloReference`, `OwnCopyReference` (`lib/data/foods/food_reference.dart`), table `food_reference`. A base catalogue kind comes later.

**Catalogue.** A source of foods to log: the base catalogue, always there, and the catalogue a connector unlocks (MyFitnessPal; Ekklo brings none). Which base catalogue is not decided.

**Meal.** One of Fūjin's own meals of a day, from a list that starts as the owner's MyFitnessPal meals. Each connector maps Fūjin's meals to its own through meal mappings. Code: `Meal` (`lib/data/meals/meal.dart`), `MealRepository` (`lib/data/meals/meal_repository.dart`), table `meal`, with a name and a position. Positions follow the order of the MyFitnessPal diary's meal names, read once per launch (`JournalService.mealNames`, then `MealRepository.arrange` in one transaction: one meal per name, found through its MyFitnessPal mapping or created, meals not named after them); a copy-in that meets another meal name creates that meal at the end.

**Connector.** Fūjin's link to one external app: MyFitnessPal, Ekklo, later Hevy. Connecting one unlocks what that app brings (its catalogue, its diary, its chat); Fūjin works with none connected. Not "integration".

**Role.** How a connector takes part in one domain (food, later workouts): two-way, where an add, edit or deletion made in that app changes Fūjin's entries on the next read, or receive-only, where a change made there only changes that app's status. Set per connector and domain in a setting, from the roles that connector can do; at most one two-way connector per domain. A change applies from the next read and rewrites nothing. In Réglages « RÔLE DES APPS · ALIMENTATION »: « Deux sens » (two-way) or « Réception » (receive-only), asked through a confirmation sheet; kept on this phone only (shared preferences, key `setting_role_<connector>_<domain>`), so a new phone starts on the defaults, MyFitnessPal two-way and Ekklo receive-only. MyFitnessPal can do both; Ekklo only receive-only, shown as « Réception » without a choice, so the rule of one two-way connector per domain holds by construction (Ekklo two-way, and with it switching the other app to receive-only, is deferred). MyFitnessPal receive-only: a row logged only in the app is neither copied in, shown nor removed; a linked row changed under its id, or gone with an unheld row of the same food on its date (paired like a replacement), keeps Fūjin's values, its link takes the row's, a replacement is recorded and the writer writes Fūjin's values back; a linked row gone unpaired loses its link and is added again; no MyFitnessPal read soft-deletes an entry, and waiting send links of rows Fūjin does not hold stay waiting. Back in two-way, the next read copies in the rows logged only in the app. Code: `Role`, `ConnectorDomain`, `ConnectorRoles` (`lib/data/connectors/`, `ConnectorRoles.offered`, `ConnectorRoles.defaultOf`), `SettingsRepository.roles` and `setRole`, `foodRolesProvider` (`lib/pages/common/food_roles_notifier.dart`), `RoleRow` (`lib/pages/settings/widgets/role_row.dart`); `compareMfpDay` and `adoptWaiting` take the MyFitnessPal role, `JournalService` reads the roles at each comparison. Not "mode", which is the automatic mode, or "direction".

**Bridge.** Carrying data from one connected app to another through Fūjin: MyFitnessPal entries to Ekklo items today, later Hevy workouts. Not "sync", which is kept for Fūjin's own data across devices.

**MyFitnessPal entry.** One food logged in a MyFitnessPal meal: food, servings, serving size, nutrients, and an entry id that changes whenever the entry is edited in MyFitnessPal. Entries without an id cannot be linked. Code: `MfpFoodEntry` from `package:myfitnesspal_client`.

**Item (Ekklo).** One food inside an Ekklo meal: Ekklo food id, quantity, quantity type (grams or portion), creation date. Ekklo stores no free text on it, so Fūjin cannot tag it. Code: `EkkloDailyMealItem` inside `EkkloDailyMeal` from `package:ekklo_client`.

**Memory (Mémoire).** What Fūjin has learned about how MyFitnessPal maps to Ekklo: remembered foods, remembered units, meal mappings and barcode links. Built by the owner, kept in SQLite, backed up. Shown on the Mémoire tab, where the owner changes the Ekklo food of a remembered food, edits unit weights, changes meal mappings, or forgets a food so the next send asks again. Code: `Memory` (`lib/data/memory/memory.dart`), `MemoryRepository` (`lib/data/memory/memory_repository.dart`), `MemoryService` (`lib/domain/memory/memory_service.dart`). Stored as foods, references, unit weights, barcode links, meals and meal mappings; `MemoryRepository` reads and writes that shape through `FoodRepository` and `MealRepository`. Forgetting stamps `deleted_at` on the rows rather than deleting them.

**Remembered food.** The Ekklo food that stands for a MyFitnessPal food. Either a matched food, an existing Ekklo food counted in grams, or an own copy ("aliment perso"), a food Fūjin created in Ekklo as an exact copy of a MyFitnessPal entry's values, tied to the serving unit and the MyFitnessPal food version it was copied from: per 100 g for a gram serving, one portion per unit otherwise. Code: sealed `RememberedFood` with `MatchedFood` and `OwnCopy` (`OwnCopy.mfpUnit`, `OwnCopy.mfpFoodVersion`) (`lib/data/memory/remembered_food.dart`), stored as a `food` with its `mfp` reference and an `ekklo` reference (matched food) or `own_copy` references (one per serving unit) in `food_reference`; the MyFitnessPal description shown is the food's name. Listed as "MÉMORISÉS" on the add search (A02a, A02b), one row per matched food and per own-copy unit, opened in the fiche in a remembered unit (`rememberedServing`, `lib/domain/adding/remembered_serving.dart`, [ADR 0034](docs/adr/0034-remembered-foods-in-the-add-search.md)).

**Barcode link.** The MyFitnessPal food the owner chose for a barcode that MyFitnessPal did not resolve to the right food: kept when a food reached from a scan through the search is added, and offered first the next time that code is scanned. One link per code; a later choice replaces it. Part of Memory, not listed on the Mémoire tab. Code: `BarcodeLink` (`lib/data/memory/barcode_link.dart`), `MemoryRepository.barcodeFood` and `MemoryRepository.saveBarcodeLink`, row `FoodBarcode` (`lib/data/foods/food_barcode.dart`), table `barcode_link`, pointing at a food. ADR 0027.

**Remembered unit.** How many grams one MyFitnessPal serving unit of a given food weighs, for matched foods logged in a unit other than grams. Code: `RememberedUnit` (`lib/data/memory/remembered_unit.dart`), stored as a `FoodUnit` in table `food_unit`.

**Meal mapping.** Which meal of a connector stands for one of Fūjin's meals: today each meal has a MyFitnessPal and an Ekklo mapping, so the pair says which Ekklo meal receives a given MyFitnessPal meal. Without one, an entry of that meal has no expected item; the first send of that meal keeps its name in Ekklo and records the mapping. Code: `MealMapping` (`lib/data/memory/meal_mapping.dart`) for the pair, `ConnectorMeal` (`lib/data/meals/connector_meal.dart`) for one row, `Connector` (`lib/data/connectors/connector.dart`), table `meal_mapping`.

**MyFitnessPal link.** Fūjin's record of the MyFitnessPal entry that holds one entry, in MyFitnessPal's terms: its MyFitnessPal entry id (which changes on every edit made there), date, meal name, MyFitnessPal food id and version, servings and serving size written. At most one live link per entry and per MyFitnessPal entry id; an add or edit whose MyFitnessPal id a read already copied in takes that copy over. Code: `MfpLink` (`lib/data/entries/mfp_link.dart`), table `mfp_link`, owned by `EntryRepository`. ADR 0044.

**Ekklo link.** Fūjin's record of the Ekklo item that holds one entry: the Ekklo meal and item ids, the entry's meal, food, servings and serving size when the item was written, and the send time (`LinkedItem`), with or without an unattended item; a link without an item carries only its unattended item. At most one live link per entry and per item. It is what Fūjin keeps about sending, and outlives its entry's deletion while its item exists (orphan link). Replaces the send link. Code: `EkkloLink`, `LinkedItem` (`lib/data/entries/`), table `ekklo_link`, owned by `EntryRepository` (`linkItem`, `refreshLink`, `unlinkItem`). ADR 0044.

**Waiting send link.** A `sent_link` row not yet adopted: written before step 2 of the connector model, or imported from a version 3, 2 or 1 backup file, keyed by MyFitnessPal entry id with the values of today's Ekklo link in MyFitnessPal terms. On the first fresh read of its day it becomes the Ekklo link of the entry holding that MyFitnessPal id, stored or copied in by that read, or, when the id has left the diary, of a deleted entry built from its values (an orphan link); the `unattended_item` rows of the day become unattended items of those links the same way, and the day's waiting rows are forgotten in the transaction that stores the read. Days never read again keep them. Code: `SentLink` (`lib/data/links/sent_link.dart`), `SentLinkRepository` (`forDate`, `forgetDate`, `all`, `replaceAll`, `lib/data/links/sent_link_repository.dart`), `UnattendedItem`, `UnattendedItemRepository` (`forDate`, `forgetDate`, `lib/data/unattended/`), `adoptWaiting` (`lib/domain/comparison/adopt_waiting.dart`). ADR 0044.

**Unattended item.** The expectation an Ekklo link carries, written before the Ekklo write, of the item an automatic send gives an entry when Memory does not predict it or the owner would have had to review it: Ekklo meal name, Ekklo food, quantity and quantity type (an item-less link when the entry has no Ekklo link yet). It lets adoption find that item after an interrupted send, and makes the linked entry "À mettre à jour" once Memory expects another item, while the entry is unchanged since its item was written. It travels with the link when an orphan link moves, and stays on the link when the link's item is gone. Forgotten when a later send writes the entry with the owner's choices, when "Revoir" keeps exactly what was sent, or when the entry is deleted (the item-less links of deleted entries are dropped). In the backup file since version 4. Code: `UnattendedExpectation` (`lib/data/entries/unattended_expectation.dart`) on `EkkloLink.unattended`, `EntryRepository.expect` and `forgetExpectations`, `ExpectedItem.unattended`; `unattended_item` rows from before step 2 wait for their day (Waiting send link). ADR 0025.

**Orphan link.** The Ekklo link of a deleted entry whose Ekklo item still exists, usually because the entry was edited in the MyFitnessPal app, which gives it a new id, so the read saw a deletion and a new entry. It is paired with an unlinked live entry of the same food, in the same meal first, then in any meal, and moves to it with its written values and its unattended item (the entry's own unattended item wins); left unpaired, it keeps its link and shows nothing. The item of an entry whose deletion MyFitnessPal still owes is never an orphan: it stays that entry's until the background writer removes it, Ekklo first. Code: `compareEkkloDay` (`lib/domain/comparison/compare_ekklo_day.dart`), `StoredDay.deleted`.

**Adoption.** Giving an unlinked live entry an Ekklo link because an unclaimed Ekklo item already matches its expected item or its unattended item, in Fūjin's order, for example after a send interrupted before its link was written. Waiting send links become Ekklo links the same way (`adoptWaiting`). Code: `compareEkkloDay`, stored as `DayChanges.addedEkkloLinks` or `changedEkkloLinks`. On the MyFitnessPal side, adoption of a written row gives a live entry without a MyFitnessPal link the diary row no link holds that has its values (meal, MyFitnessPal food, servings, serving value and unit; oldest entry first): the row a write left unlinked when the app was killed or the reply was lost. A read runs it before copy-in, and the background writer runs it before every add or copy, so MyFitnessPal is never written twice. Code: `writtenRow` (`lib/domain/comparison/written_row.dart`), `compareMfpDay`, `BackgroundWriter`.

**Relink.** Moving an orphan link to the entry it pairs with: the same row on the new entry, `sent_at` kept. When the item already matches what the entry now expects, the link's written values are refreshed and the entry is "Dans Ekklo"; a linked entry changed in Fūjin is refreshed the same way. Code: `compareEkkloDay`, `LinkedItem.refreshedFrom`, `DayChanges.changedEkkloLinks`.

**Status.** Where an entry stands in Ekklo, recomputed on every read from Fūjin's entries, the Ekklo day and the entries' Ekklo links: "Dans Ekklo" (`InEkklo`, carries its Ekklo link), "À envoyer" (`ToSend`), "À mettre à jour" (`ToUpdate`, carries the Ekklo link, moved from an orphan or of an entry changed since its item was written, or of an unchanged entry with an unattended item whose item Memory no longer expects, and an `UpdateKind`: `quantityOnly` when the Ekklo meal is unchanged, `mealChanged` otherwise). A row without a Fūjin id, a MyFitnessPal entry without an id or an entry a held read would copy in, is "À envoyer" with no swipe and no fiche, and is never sent. Code: sealed `EntryStatus` (`lib/domain/comparison/entry_status.dart`), `UpdateKind` (`lib/domain/comparison/update_kind.dart`), `ComparedEntry.entryId` (null for such a row). An entry dated another day than its MyFitnessPal link (a change of day not yet written) is "À mettre à jour" (`mealChanged`) and keeps its Ekklo link. The MyFitnessPal status is separate: see MyFitnessPal status. A later step of the connector model (not built, ADR 0044) adds Ekklo "to remove", for an entry deleted in Fūjin or in MyFitnessPal whose item is still in Ekklo, removed by the next reviewed send.

**Connector status.** Whether a Journal row still waits for one of the apps, shown the same way for both: the connector pill ("MFP" or "Ekklo" and a clock, outlined in that app's colour, `role/source/mfp` or `role/source/ekklo`), whose hand turns while the row waits and stands still on a failure or when animations are reduced (Figma "Expérience 5"). MyFitnessPal: none once MyFitnessPal holds the entry as Fūjin has it, pending (`MfpPending`) while it has a pending write, its clock stopped while the background writer's last try for it failed (`mfpFailedIds`), until the next try; its pill comes first. Ekklo: while the writer holds an `EkkloSend` for the entry, its pill replaces "À envoyer" or "À mettre à jour" (never "Dans Ekklo"), still while the entry has a write failure; without a held job the Ekklo status is shown as usual. A write that failed because the phone is offline is not a failure here: the clock keeps turning until the network comes back (`mfpFailedIds` and `failedIds` leave out `SendFailure.network`). Offline, the Journal shows the MyFitnessPal pill only, since Ekklo is not read. Not part of `EntryStatus`. Code: sealed `MfpStatus` (`lib/domain/comparison/mfp_status.dart`), `ComparedEntry.mfp`, `WriterState.held`, `ConnectorPill` (`lib/pages/journal/widgets/connector_pill.dart`), `mfpFailedIds`, `failedIds`, `ekkloSendIds` (`lib/pages/journal/writer_state_ids.dart`). ADR 0044.

**Pending write (in MyFitnessPal).** What MyFitnessPal still owes an entry, derived from Fūjin's tables on each look, with no queue: an add (`MfpAdd`, a live entry without a live MyFitnessPal link), an edit (`MfpEdit`, a live entry whose date, MyFitnessPal meal, MyFitnessPal food, servings or serving differ from its live link; values compared, never timestamps) or an owed deletion (`MfpDeletion`). Code: sealed `MfpWrite` (`lib/data/entries/mfp_write.dart`), `EntryRepository.pendingMfp`. ADR 0044.

**Owed deletion.** A soft-deleted entry whose MyFitnessPal link is still live: deleted in Fūjin (or an add undone) but not yet removed from MyFitnessPal. A removal no longer soft-deletes the link; the background writer does, once MyFitnessPal no longer has the row. Reads hold its MyFitnessPal id (never copied in again) and forget the link when the row has left the diary. Code: `MfpDeletion`, `StoredDay.owed`.

**Owed deletion row.** A Journal row for an entry deleted in Fūjin that an app still holds: an owed deletion, or an entry whose Ekklo removal is held. It stays in its meal, greyed (name, kcal and serving in tertiary text), with the detail "Supprimé dans Fūjin" and the connector pill of each app that still holds it, until both let go of it. No swipe, no fiche; it counts in no total, status count or goal ring (Figma 555:23742). Code: `DayDraft.leaving`, `DayComparison.leaving`, `JournalMeal.leaving`, `DiaryMeal.leaving` (offline), `MealCard`, `StoredMealCard`, `MealRows`.

**Background writer.** What writes MyFitnessPal, then Ekklo, after Fūjin saved an add, an edit or a deletion. A run takes every pending write, all days, oldest entry first, one at a time, each followed by its entry's held Ekklo job, in the order of ADR 0002: add = MyFitnessPal add, then Ekklo; edit = (change of day with an Ekklo item: Ekklo removal), MyFitnessPal copy, removal of the original, link moved, then Ekklo; deletion = Ekklo removal, MyFitnessPal removal, link soft-deleted. Before writing it re-reads the food and writes the current MyFitnessPal version at the same serving (version moved: entry nutrients refreshed and a stale own copy renewed; serving gone: a write failure). Right after an add's or an edit's MyFitnessPal write, when nothing is held for the entry, it derives the Ekklo send from Memory alone (derived Ekklo send). Runs in the app process only, after each save, on return to the foreground, after each Journal read from a pull or a change of day, and when the network comes back (`connectivityProvider`, from `connectivity_plus`, not connected to connected); the return to the foreground and the network coming back read the Journal's day first and run the writer from that read; single flight, a call during a run asks for one more run after it; no retry loop: a failure stays until "Réessayer" or the next trigger. A deletion whose entry still has a live Ekklo item removes that item first, held removal or not, so it survives a restart. On its own diary read the writer re-reads the stored link and, when the link's row is gone, pairs it with the row the MyFitnessPal app made (replacement) before writing. A run stops at the first write that failed because the network is down (a network exception on a read request, `networkDown`), leaving later entries untouched and pending. Code: `BackgroundWriter` (`lib/domain/writing/background_writer.dart`), `networkDown` (`lib/domain/writing/network_down.dart`), `WriterState` (`WriterIdle`, `WriterRunning`), `BackgroundWriteNotifier` (`lib/pages/journal/background_write_notifier.dart`), `JournalNotifier.reload(thenWrite:)`. ADR 0044.

**Held Ekklo job.** The Ekklo side the owner reviewed for one entry, kept in memory by the background writer until it runs after the entry's MyFitnessPal write: the fiche's planned entry for an add or an edit (`EkkloSend`, held only when it sends) or the removal of its item for a deletion (`EkkloRemoval`). Never stored: lost when the app is killed, the entry is then simply "À envoyer" (or keeps its item, for a deletion), unless a derived Ekklo send takes it over after a MyFitnessPal write still owed. A held job always wins over a derived send. A derived send that failed is held the same way, so "Réessayer" and later runs retry it while the app lives. Code: sealed `EkkloJob` (`lib/domain/writing/ekklo_job.dart`), `BackgroundWriter.hold`.

**Write failure.** A background write that did not go through: `MfpNotWritten` (the row's MyFitnessPal pill stops; snackbar "Pas écrit dans MyFitnessPal", "{aliment} reste en attente", "Réessayer") or `EkkloNotWritten` (the row's Ekklo pill stops; snackbar "Pas écrit dans Ekklo" with the same detail and "Réessayer"). Offline is waiting, not failure: every write, a deletion included, starts with a MyFitnessPal read, so the run stops at that read, with no snackbar, the row's clock still turning, until the network comes back; a write blocked in Fūjin DEV is a failure and shows the snackbar without "Réessayer". Kept in the writer's state until the entry's next try or until it no longer has a pending write. Code: sealed `WriteFailure` (`lib/domain/writing/write_failure.dart`), `JournalSnackBar.written`.

**Derived Ekklo send.** The Ekklo send the background writer works out for an entry right after its MyFitnessPal add or edit lands, when nothing is held for it: the entry's plan from Memory alone (`planEntry` with no network), sent only when its Ekklo side is "mémorisé" (a matched Ekklo food with known grams, or a fresh own copy reused). It survives a restart, since it is derived from the stored entry; an entry copied in from the MyFitnessPal app owes MyFitnessPal nothing, so it never goes by itself. Every other entry waits for "Envoyer vers Ekklo" and its review. Code: `derivedSend` (`lib/domain/writing/derived_send.dart`), `BackgroundWriter`. ADR 0044.

**Replacement (Remplacé dans MyFitnessPal).** What a fresh read stores when Fūjin's unwritten change meets a change made meanwhile in the MyFitnessPal app, which Fūjin's change wins (with MyFitnessPal receive-only, any change the app made to a linked entry, as if Fūjin's values were a pending edit): the same MyFitnessPal entry with other values under a pending edit, or the linked entry gone (the MyFitnessPal app gave it a new id) under a pending edit or an owed deletion, its link then moved to a diary row of the same food on the link's date that no link holds, the same meal first, oldest entry first; the writer then copies Fūjin's values, or removes that row. The Journal says it once per read in a snackbar: "Remplacé dans MyFitnessPal", "{aliment} : {avant} → {après}" (the MyFitnessPal app's amount, then Fūjin's; the meal names when only the meal differs), "{aliment} supprimé dans Fūjin" for a deletion, "{n} aliments gardés comme dans Fūjin" for several. A held read never shows it; the background writer, pairing on its own diary read, announces its replacements the same way at the end of its run (`WriterIdle.replaced`). Code: sealed `Replacement` (`ReplacedEdit`, `ReplacedDeletion`, `lib/domain/comparison/replacement.dart`), `replacingRow` (`lib/domain/comparison/replacing_row.dart`), `compareMfpDay`, `JournalService.settleDay`, `BackgroundWriter.settled`, `WriterIdle.replaced`, `JournalSnackBar.written`. ADR 0044.

**Expected item.** The Ekklo item Memory predicts for an entry: Ekklo meal name, Ekklo food id, quantity rounded to 0.1, quantity type. Null when the entry's food has no MyFitnessPal reference, or Memory lacks the food, the meal mapping, or the grams of a non-gram unit. Matches an item within 0.1. Code: `ExpectedItem.forEntry` (on a `LoggedEntry`) and `ExpectedItem.matches` (`lib/domain/comparison/expected_item.dart`).

**Day comparison.** The result of comparing one day, in three pure steps: adopting the day's waiting send links (`adoptWaiting`), the MyFitnessPal side, by its role (`compareMfpDay`: copy-in, update, delete when two-way; Fūjin's values kept and put back when receive-only), then the Ekklo side, receive-only (`compareEkkloDay`: statuses, adoption, relink, drop). It holds each row of the day with its status, and the changes to store: entries and MyFitnessPal links to add, change or delete, Ekklo links to add, move, refresh or drop, and whether the day's waiting rows are done. A fresh read stores those changes in one transaction (`EntryRepository.store`); a comparison composed from held reads stores nothing and shows the entries it would copy in as rows without a Fūjin id (`DayComparison.held`). Code: `DayComparison` (`lib/domain/comparison/day_comparison.dart`), `ComparedEntry` (`lib/domain/comparison/compared_entry.dart`), `compareDay` (`lib/domain/comparison/compare_day.dart`), `DayDraft` (the day between steps), `DayChanges` (`lib/data/entries/day_changes.dart`).

**Journal day.** Everything the Journal shows for one day: the rows of the day comparison, Fūjin's meals holding them (the diary's meal names in order, then other meals holding a row by position), the Ekklo meals, with derived counts and nutrient totals. Code: `JournalDay` (`lib/domain/journal/journal_day.dart`), `JournalMeal` (`lib/domain/journal/journal_meal.dart`), built by `JournalService.readDay` and `JournalService.journalOf` (`lib/domain/journal/journal_service.dart`).

**Journal read.** What one read of the Journal got: both apps (`BothSides`, carrying the journal day), MyFitnessPal only (`MfpOnly`, Fūjin's entries of the day without status, by meal, and why Ekklo failed) or Ekklo only (`EkkloOnly`, the Ekklo meals and why MyFitnessPal failed), with the time of the read. Fūjin only (`FujinOnly`) when MyFitnessPal is offline, or Ekklo is offline while MyFitnessPal was read: Fūjin's stored entries of the day and its owed deletions, in Fūjin's meals by position, composed from `fujin.db` alone and storing nothing. When neither app answers otherwise, the read fails and the Journal keeps the previous read under the problem card. Code: sealed `JournalRead` (`lib/domain/journal/journal_read.dart`), built by `JournalService.journalOf` from the two held reads of the day (`Fetch`, `lib/domain/journal/fetch.dart`). ADR 0022, ADR 0029.

**Held read.** One side of one day as last read for display, kept in memory (never in `fujin.db`) for 5 minutes after the last screen stops showing it: either the value with its read time (`Fetched`) or the failure (`FetchFailed`), held alike. The Journal composes a held day at once, without skeletons, then reads it again in the background; the week band draws its rings from held diaries only and reads the settled week and its two neighbours ahead. Composing writes nothing: a day's comparison is stored only from reads just made or waited for (`JournalService.settleDay`): both sides from a fresh pair, the MyFitnessPal side alone from a fresh diary when Ekklo failed, nothing when MyFitnessPal failed. A held diary can predate an add or an edit, so storing it would delete the entry just written; week reads therefore store nothing. Everything that writes reads both sides live instead (`JournalService.readDay`). Code: sealed `Fetch` with `Fetched` and `FetchFailed` (`lib/domain/journal/fetch.dart`), `mfpDiaryProvider`, `ekkloMealsProvider`, `weekReadProvider` (`lib/pages/journal/journal_notifier.dart`), `JournalService.journalOf`. ADR 0029.

**Read problem.** Why one side of a Journal read failed, from the client exception: a MyFitnessPal or Ekklo session expired, offline, MyFitnessPal or Ekklo not responding, or unknown. It picks the problem card. Code: `ReadProblem` (`lib/domain/journal/read_problem.dart`), `JournalProblemCard` (`lib/pages/journal/widgets/journal_problem_card.dart`).

**Day nutrients.** Kilocalories, protein, carbohydrates, fat and fiber added up over a day, from Fūjin's entries as logged (`DayNutrients.ofEntries`, with the nutrients some entries lack, shown as "≥") or from Ekklo meals (manual meal values first). Code: `DayNutrients` (`lib/domain/journal/day_nutrients.dart`).

**Goal status.** How an amount compares with its goal: below under 98 %, reached within 2 %, exceeded beyond 102 %; fiber is a floor and never exceeded. A day of the week band adds upcoming, no goal, nothing logged, in progress (today) and missed (a past day below). Code: `GoalStatus` (`lib/domain/goals/goal_status.dart`), `DayGoalState` (`lib/domain/goals/day_goal_state.dart`). ADR 0022.

**Status counts.** How many entries of a day or meal are in Ekklo, to send, to update and being sent by the background writer (`sending`); `pending` is the last three (not the MyFitnessPal pending of `MfpPending`). They feed "N/M dans Ekklo" and the send button label. Code: `StatusCounts` (`lib/domain/journal/status_counts.dart`).

**Refresh outcome.** What a pull to refresh found: `changed`, or `nothingNew` when the MyFitnessPal diary is identical before and after, which triggers the "Rien de nouveau" snackbar. Code: `RefreshOutcome` (`lib/pages/journal/refresh_outcome.dart`).

**Send plan.** What a send will do for one day, built before anything is written: one planned entry per entry "À envoyer" or "À mettre à jour" with a Fūjin id, keyed by it, the entries still being searched, and the entries already in Ekklo. Code: `SendPlan` (`lib/domain/sending/send_plan.dart`), built by `SendService.plan` (`lib/domain/sending/send_service.dart`) from the pure rules in `planEntry` (`lib/domain/sending/plan_entry.dart`).

**Planned entry.** One entry of a send plan: its Ekklo meal, its choice (an Ekklo food with grams, an own copy to reuse or create, or skipped), its ranked candidates, whether it is reviewed and confirmed, and the link of the item it updates. Code: `PlannedEntry` (`lib/domain/sending/planned_entry.dart`), sealed `SendChoice` (`SendToEkkloFood`, `SendAsOwnCopy`, `SkipEntry`, `lib/domain/sending/send_choice.dart`).

**Automatic / to review.** A planned entry is automatic ("Mémorisé") when Memory fully decides it; an automatic new own copy says why instead: "Nouvelle unité" when the food has an own copy only in another unit, "Aliment modifié" when its own copy is stale (`OwnCopyRenewal`, `lib/domain/sending/own_copy_renewal.dart`); every other one is to review ("à vérifier") on screen 10 and shows why: "Poids à confirmer", "Nouveau choix", "Aucun aliment proche", then "Confirmé" or "Sauté". Code: `PlannedEntry.reviewed`, `ReviewReason` (`lib/domain/sending/review_reason.dart`).

**Automatic mode (Mode automatique).** A Réglages setting, off by default and kept on this phone only (shared preferences), under which "Envoyer vers Ekklo" skips screen 10 and sends every proposal as is, then shows screen 17. Such a send remembers nothing but the own copies it creates; the entries that would have been to review are the "choix faits sans toi", each with "Revoir" to keep its choice in Memory. Not to be confused with an automatic planned entry ("Mémorisé"). Code: `Setting.automaticMode` (`lib/data/settings/setting.dart`), `SettingsRepository` (`lib/data/settings/settings_repository.dart`), `automaticModeProvider` (`lib/pages/common/automatic_mode_notifier.dart`), `SendMode` (`lib/domain/sending/send_mode.dart`), `SendService.keep`. ADR 0025.

**Appearance (Apparence).** A Réglages setting that picks the theme: "Système" (the default, follows the phone), "Clair" or "Sombre". Kept on this phone only (shared preferences), not in the backup file. The native launch screen always follows the phone. The day/night toggle ("Bascule jour/nuit"), a glass drop pinned at the top right of the Journal, Mémoire and Réglages while the page scrolls under it, is a shortcut: a moon on the light theme on screen, a sun on the dark one; a tap sets "Sombre" or "Clair", leaving "Système", which is only chosen back in Réglages. A change of theme spreads as a circle from the control touched (the centre of the screen when the phone itself switches). Code: `Appearance` (`lib/data/settings/appearance.dart`), `SettingsRepository.appearance`, `appearanceProvider` (`lib/pages/common/appearance_notifier.dart`), `AppearanceRow` (`lib/pages/settings/widgets/appearance_row.dart`), `AppearanceToggle`, `AppearanceCorner`, `AppearanceHeader`, `ThemeReveal` (`lib/pages/common/`), colours in `FujinColors`. ADR 0035.

**Candidate.** An Ekklo food proposed for a MyFitnessPal entry, with the grams to send, whether they were estimated from the kilocalories, the nutrient gaps and how the names match. Acceptable when a name or brand word is shared and the gaps are within tolerance (kcal 12 %, each macro 10 % or 5 kcal). Code: `EkkloCandidate` (`lib/domain/sending/ekklo_candidate.dart`), `NutrientDeltas` (`lib/domain/sending/nutrient_deltas.dart`), `NameMatch`, `rankCandidates` (`lib/domain/sending/rank_candidates.dart`).

**Unit weight.** How a planned Ekklo food got the grams of a non-gram serving: remembered, estimated from the kilocalories, or confirmed by the owner (screen 12). Estimated and confirmed weights become remembered units when the send runs. Code: `UnitWeight` (`lib/domain/sending/unit_weight.dart`).

**Send step.** One write of a send, shown on screens 14 and 16: create an own copy, update a quantity in place, or send one Ekklo meal. Code: sealed `SendStep` (`OwnCopyStep`, `QuantityUpdateStep`, `MealStep`, `lib/domain/sending/send_step.dart`), `SendProgress`, `StepState`.

**Send report.** What screen 15 sums up: foods sent, remembered foods reused, new associations, retained unit weights, own copies created, updates; for an automatic send, what screen 17 lists: the send mode and the planned entries decided without the owner. Code: `SendReport` (`lib/domain/sending/send_report.dart`).

**Add draft.** What the add or edit fiche (A04, A08) edits before it is saved: a MyFitnessPal food, one of its serving sizes, the servings, the MyFitnessPal meal and the day. Its entry is the entry the fiche plans and shows, carrying empty ids until it is stored; its MyFitnessPal entry (`mfpEntry`) is what the background writer will log, nutrients scaled from the food's. The writer rebuilds it from the stored entry and the food read again (`AddDraft.forEntry`, the same serving value and unit, else none). Offline, the fiche builds it from the food on the phone. Code: `AddDraft` (`lib/domain/adding/add_draft.dart`). ADR 0026.

**Ekklo side.** The Ekklo summary line of the add fiche, from the draft's planned entry: "mémorisé" (`remembered`), "trouvé automatiquement" (`foundAutomatically`), "sera créé depuis MFP" (`willBeCreated`), "à choisir" (`toChoose`, an estimated unit weight to confirm before adding), "choisi" (`chosen`). When the plan needs Ekklo and Ekklo cannot be reached, the fiche has no plan and says "à vérifier à l’envoi": the food can still be saved with « Ajouter à MFP » (« Enregistrer dans MFP » on the edit fiche, without the write-order line), with no Ekklo send held, and waits for "Envoyer vers Ekklo"; the card has no chevron then. Code: `EkkloSide` (`lib/domain/adding/ekklo_side.dart`), planned by `AddFoodService.plan` with the send flow's `planEntry`.

**Added food.** The result of adding a food from Fūjin, handed back to the Journal as soon as the entry is saved in Fūjin: the stored entry, with its Fūjin id, and its planned entry, whose Ekklo send is held for the background writer; no planned entry when the fiche could not plan Ekklo offline. Its undo is a removal. Code: `AddedFood` (`lib/domain/adding/added_food.dart`), `AddFoodService.add` (`lib/domain/adding/add_food_service.dart`). ADR 0026.

**Removal.** Taking a logged food out of both apps, from the undo of an add (K17) or from the Journal's "Supprimer" (K24, K25): the entry soft-deleted in Fūjin at once, with any item-less Ekklo link, its MyFitnessPal link left live, so the deletion is owed; an Ekklo removal is held when the entry has a live item. The background writer then removes the Ekklo item (its link then keeps only its unattended item, or is dropped), the MyFitnessPal entry, and soft-deletes the MyFitnessPal link. It cannot fail in Fūjin; a failure of either app is a write failure. Code: `RemoveEntryService` (`lib/domain/editing/remove_entry_service.dart`), `RemoveEntryNotifier` (`lib/pages/journal/remove_entry_notifier.dart`), `EntryRemoval`, `RemovalReason`. ADR 0043.

**Edit of a logged food.** Changing the serving, quantity, meal or day of a Journal row from the fiche in edit mode: the entry takes the new values in Fūjin at once, keeping its Fūjin id, and the fiche closes; the edit is then pending. MyFitnessPal has no edit, so the background writer adds a copy there and removes the original (the MyFitnessPal entry gets a new id), and the same MyFitnessPal link row takes the copy's id and values; then Ekklo is updated with the held send; a change of day removes the Ekklo item first. "Annuler" (revert) is another edit saved first. An entry still waiting for its MyFitnessPal add can be edited too; only Fūjin's entry changes. Code: `EditEntryService` (`lib/domain/editing/edit_entry_service.dart`), `EditOutcome` (`Edited`, `NotEdited`), `EditedFood`, `EkkloEdit`. ADR 0043.

**Adding possible.** Whether a food can be added now, decided from the latest Journal read of the selected day: not when that read failed or lacks a side because a MyFitnessPal or Ekklo session expired; yes otherwise, offline and a service merely not responding included, since the add is saved in Fūjin and the background writer writes the apps later. It greys the tab bar's Scanner button on all three tabs and "Ajouter" on screen 19. Code: `addingPossible` (`lib/domain/adding/adding_possible.dart`), `AddShortcut.possible`. ADR 0027.

**Meal of the hour.** The MyFitnessPal meal a scan from the tab bar, or "Ajouter" on screen 19, starts on (`AddShortcut.mealOfNow`), by its position in the diary's meal names: the first (breakfast) before 11:00, the second (lunch) from 11:00, the fourth (snacks) from 16:00, the third (dinner) from 18:00; null when the diary has fewer meals, and the first meal is taken then. When MyFitnessPal's meal names cannot be read, Fūjin's stored meals stand in, by position (`JournalService.storedMealNames`); the fiche's meal menu does the same. Code: `mealOfTheHour` (`lib/domain/adding/meal_of_the_hour.dart`). ADR 0027.

**Scan outcome.** What a barcode lookup found: a food (`ScanFound`, `remembered` when it came from a barcode link rather than MyFitnessPal), a code MyFitnessPal knows without a food card, with its name and brand (`ScanUnresolved`, A03d), or an unknown code (`ScanUnknown`, A03e). The barcode link is tried first, MyFitnessPal's lookup when there is none or its food is gone; a failed lookup throws instead. Code: sealed `ScanOutcome` (`lib/domain/adding/scan_outcome.dart`), `AddFoodService.scan`. ADR 0027.

**Store code (Code magasin).** A barcode a retailer assigns to its own products: 13 digits starting with 02 or 20 to 29, or 12 digits starting with 2. The same code can name different products in different shops, so the found food sheet warns that it may not be the owner's (A03h). Code: `isStoreCode` (`lib/domain/adding/store_code.dart`). ADR 0027.

**Recent food.** A MyFitnessPal food Fūjin added, kept on this phone with its last serving, servings, kilocalories and time, shown as "Récents" on the add search (A02a), newest first, at most 20; a remembered food already there is not listed again under "MÉMORISÉS" (the recent of a remembered food: `recentOf`, `lib/domain/adding/recent_of.dart`), and its serving unit is the first one a remembered food opens in (`rememberedServing`, `lib/domain/adding/remembered_serving.dart`). Not in the backup file, and kept through an import. Its name and brand are those of its food, kept current by each recent recorded. Code: `RecentFood` (`lib/data/recents/recent_food.dart`), `RecentFoodRow`, `RecentFoodRepository` (`lib/data/recents/recent_food_repository.dart`), table `recent_food`, one row per food. ADR 0026.

**Session.** The credentials a client needs: Ekklo tokens or MyFitnessPal session cookies, kept in secure storage, never backed up. Code: `SecureEkkloTokenStore`, `SecureMfpSessionStore`, `SessionKey` (`lib/data/sessions/`).

**Connected accounts.** Whether a session is stored for MyFitnessPal and for Ekklo; it does not ask either service whether the session still works. It decides between the welcome screen and the Journal. Code: `ConnectedAccounts`, `AccountsService` (`lib/domain/accounts/`).

**Goals.** The owner's daily nutrition targets, the same every day: kilocalories (required) and optionally protein, carbohydrates, fat and fiber, all above zero. Stored by Fūjin, not MyFitnessPal, in one row of the `goals` table, with the time it was last saved (`updated_at`). The kilocalories of the macros (P×4 + C×4 + F×9) are compared with the kilocalorie goal for information only. Code: `Goals` (`lib/data/goals/goals.dart`), `GoalsRepository` (`lib/data/goals/goals_repository.dart`), `MacroEnergy` (`lib/domain/goals/macro_energy.dart`), `goalsProvider` (`lib/pages/common/goals_notifier.dart`). ADR 0020.

**Backup file.** A JSON file `fujin-backup-YYYY-MM-DD.json` the owner exports and imports from Réglages: format `fujin-backup` version 4 with the rows of the foods, servings, unit weights, references, barcode links, meals, meal mappings, entries, MyFitnessPal links and Ekklo links (forgotten rows included, unattended items with their links), the waiting send links and goals, never sessions, recent foods nor `unattended_item` rows. A version 3 file leaves no entry: its send links wait in `sent_link` and are adopted on each day's next read. A version 2 file (Memory with barcode links) and a version 1 file (without barcode links) still import, turned into those rows by the rules of the database migration, their send links waiting likewise. Import is strict and replaces everything in one transaction after a confirmation. The import preview's "N liens d'envoi" counts the Ekklo links with an item and the waiting send links (`Backup.linkCount`). Code: sealed `Backup` with `BackupV4`, `BackupV3` (read only) and `BackupV2` (`lib/data/backup/backup.dart`), `BackupCodec` (`lib/data/backup/backup_codec.dart`), `BackupRepository` (`lib/data/backup/backup_repository.dart`), `BackupService` (`lib/domain/backup/backup_service.dart`), `BackupFiles` (`lib/data/backup/backup_files.dart`). ADR 0021, version 2 in ADR 0027, versions 3 and 4 in ADR 0044.

**Fake backends.** The test stand-in for both services: an `http` `MockClient` that serves MyFitnessPal and Ekklo routes from in-memory data and records requests, MyFitnessPal food search, food reads, barcode lookups and diary writes included. For runs on a device, separate local fake servers play the same role through `--dart-define`. Code: `FakeBackends` (`test/support/fake_backends.dart`).
